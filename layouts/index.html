Started by Fabio Pietrosanti

Hacking Biology was started by Fabio Pietrosanti — a hacker and entrepreneur who has spent twenty-five years building things people have to be able to trust, and who now runs longevity protocols on himself and publishes the measurements, good and bad. The Foundation is the institutional form of that habit: do the experiment, show the numbers, keep it open.

The software: biohack.it

biohack.it

The open-source platform the Foundation stewards. Biohackers document a protocol in structured form — what they take or do, dose, timing, cycles — measure its effect and its safety through biomarkers, and share protocol and results publicly.

biohack.it →

The Foundation

A tool can be forked, sold, or captured. A steward with a mission lock is what makes the promise permanent: never commercial, open by code and by data, harm reduction first, and the record belongs to the person it describes.
