## The project

Hacking Biology is a data-driven applied aging research project. The approach is
simple to state and slow to do: pick an intervention with plausible evidence behind
it, run it as a documented protocol, measure the effect with biomarkers, and publish
what the numbers say.

The posts here cover HBOT, rapamycin, epigenetic and biological-age measurement,
senolytics, blood panels, and the practical logistics of running several protocols
at once.

## Related work

**biohack.it** is the software side of the same idea: an open-source platform where
biohackers document a protocol in structured form and measure its efficacy and safety
through biomarkers, sharing protocol and results publicly.

## A necessary warning

Nothing on this site is medical advice. These are personal experiments, reported
so that other people can see the reasoning and the measurements — including when
they contradict the expectation.
