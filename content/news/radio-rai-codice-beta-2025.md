Rai's **[Codice Beta — Ogni cosa è digitale](https://www.raiplaysound.it/audio/2025/01/Codice-Beta-S04EP08-Io-biohacker-che-testo-sul-mio-corpo-i-segreti-della-longevita-42dd7997-10f4-4587-8767-e51e5fec0fbb.html)**
devoted an episode to biohacking — season 4, episode 8, *"Io, biohacker, che testo sul mio corpo i
segreti della longevità"* — with **Fabio Pietrosanti** on the protocol he runs on himself.

Radio suits this subject better than television does. There is no startling image to cut to, so
the conversation has to go through the reasoning instead: ancient practices such as ice baths and
breathwork, lifestyle interventions like consistent exercise, sleep, caloric restriction and
intermittent fasting — and then the part that actually distinguishes biohacking from wellness,
which is the technology used to **measure** whether any of it does anything.

That is the whole argument in one sentence. Cold water and fasting are not new. Knowing what they
did to your inflammatory markers, your lipids, your methylation age — on a schedule, in a form
that can be compared with what somebody else got — is new, and it is what the work documented on
[the blog](/posts/) and the platform at [biohack.it](https://biohack.it) exist to make ordinary.
