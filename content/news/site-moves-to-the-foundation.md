description: "The site leaves WordPress.com and is rebuilt as the home of the Hacking Biology Foundation, with the research blog kept intact at its original addresses."
---

hackingbiology.com has left WordPress.com. The site is now a static, open build whose entire
source — content, layout and history — lives in a
[public repository](https://github.com/hackingbiology/hackingbiology.com).

The reason is the same one that governs everything else here: the Foundation asks people to trust
an open platform with their health record, so its own publishing should be inspectable rather than
sitting inside someone's hosted account.

Nothing that was published has moved. **Every address the blog had since July 2023 still resolves
to the same article**, images included, and the feed keeps working. What is new is the structure
around it: this site now carries [what the Foundation is and guarantees](/foundation/), while the
[blog](/posts/) continues as the working record of applied longevity research.
