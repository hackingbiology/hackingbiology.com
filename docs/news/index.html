Announcements from the Foundation — governance, milestones, and the development of biohack.it . Research writing lives in the blog .

  • 18 August 2026

    hackingbiology.com becomes the Foundation's site

    The site leaves WordPress.com and is rebuilt as the home of the Hacking Biology Foundation, with the research blog kept intact at its original addresses.

  • 8 April 2026

    Hacking Biology at the Fund Longevity rally in Rome

    Hacking Biology took part in the global Fund Longevity rally on 8 April 2026, at Largo di Torre Argentina in Rome — one of twenty cities asking governments to treat aging as the public-health priority it is.

  • 2 July 2025

    Hacking Biology at the 2025 Longevity Summit, Dublin

    Fabio Pietrosanti attended the 2025 Longevity Summit at Trinity College Dublin, 2–4 July, alongside the wider longevity research and advocacy community.

  • 27 June 2025

    Biohacking on Rai 1: Fabio Pietrosanti interviewed on Codice

    Italy's main public television channel devoted an episode of Codice — La vita è digitale to biohacking, interviewing Fabio Pietrosanti about the Hacking Biology protocol and the sixty-plus pills a day it involves.

  • 17 May 2025

    Radio Rai: "Io, biohacker, che testo sul mio corpo i segreti della longevità"

    A long-form radio interview on Rai's Codice Beta — Ogni cosa è digitale, with Fabio Pietrosanti on biohacking and on the protocol he runs and measures on himself.

  • 5 January 2025

    Speciale Tg1: "Pionieri di longevità — Biohacker"

    Rai 1's Speciale Tg1, by Barbara Carfagna, devoted a report to biohacking and longevity, following the practices of Fabio Pietrosanti — known as naif — as one of the field's pioneers in Italy.

  • 22 August 2024

    Hacking Biology is presented to the Rapamycin News community

    The project was introduced to the Rapamycin News longevity community with a 14-slide presentation: measure the hallmarks of aging with biomarkers, document the protocol, and build an open platform so results can be compared across people.
