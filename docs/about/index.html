The person behind the project

About Fabio

Fabio Pietrosanti started Hacking Biology: applied longevity research run on himself, measured with biomarkers, and published in full — including what did not work.

Fabio Pietrosanti started Hacking Biology, and writes everything on this blog.

The method is the same one he has applied to other things for twenty-five years: take the claim apart, build the thing, measure it, and publish the result where it can be checked. Applied to his own body, that means running longevity protocols — HBOT, rapamycin, epigenetic and biological-age panels, senolytics, blood work — on a documented schedule, measuring what they do, and writing it down when the numbers disappoint as readily as when they cooperate.

That habit is why the Foundation exists in the form it does. If the interesting part of biohacking is the measurement, then the infrastructure that holds the measurements has to be open, inspectable, and impossible to quietly close — hence biohack.it under AGPL, stewarded by a non-profit that cannot sell it.
